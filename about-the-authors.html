ABOUT THE AUTHORS

Boniface Mutua Muthini

Systems Architect & Co-Founder

Judith Mbinya Wanga

Co-Founder & Author

Boniface Mutua Muthini and Judith Mbinya Wanga are the co-founders of MindReset Architect Books, a publishing platform dedicated to empowering readers with practical financial knowledge and life strategies. This is also part of their MindReset Architect YouTube Channel brand.

Their journey began in the classroom, where both served as educators between 2005 and 2010. This shared background in teaching instilled in them a deep commitment to making complex information accessible, clear, and actionable. Transitioning from education into the fast-paced world of digital marketing, business entrepreneurship, and investment, they have spent the years since applying these lessons in the real economy. Their combined experience bridges the gap between theory and practice, offering readers strategies that are not just mathematically sound but realistic for everyday life.
