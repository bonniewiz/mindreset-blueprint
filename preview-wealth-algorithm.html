The Wealth Algorithm

Systematizing Capital Allocation for the Passive Investor

System Diagnostic

Real estate is typically marketed as an emotional, active business. Relying on "gut feelings" or active property management creates a low-wage, high-friction second job. This structural error caps wealth generation and exposes investors to unnecessary market risks.

The Wealth Algorithm replaces emotion with a strict data-driven protocol. By treating multifamily real estate as a high-yield capital allocation system, analytical professionals can automate asset filtering, enforce binary underwriting metrics, and decouple their time from property management entirely.

The Architectural Blueprint

This framework maps the exact institutional execution standard for tax-advantaged capital deployment:

  • Capital Allocation & Algorithmic Underwriting Transition to high-velocity capital allocation via GP/LP syndication. Establish a mandatory 1.25x DSCR floor and a 5-step stress-test protocol to eliminate subjective bias.
  • Tax-Shielded Depreciation Deploy Cost Segregation Studies and bonus depreciation to harvest Passive Activity Losses (PALs), utilizing REPS or PIGs to offset taxable income.
  • The Infinite Deferral Execute the "Swap 'til you Drop" loop using 1031 Exchanges and Delaware Statutory Trusts (DSTs) to compound principal indefinitely and eliminate tax drag.
  • Structural Integrity & Execution Isolate property liability using Series LLCs (SLLCs). Implement closed-loop SOPs for institutional deal-flow, ROE audits, and automated capital redeployment.
